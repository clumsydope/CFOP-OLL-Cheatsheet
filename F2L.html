3x3 CFOP Method · First Two Layers

F2L Slot Guide

Solve a corner-and-edge pair while keeping the completed first layer intact. Match the pair to a common position, use the U layer to set it up, and insert it directly.

F2L Key

Slot — one corner-edge pair between two centers
Pair — solved together, not one piece at a time
Lookahead — inspect the next slot while turning
1. Paired above the slot R U R'

The pair is already together. Turn U to align it over the matching slot, then insert with the standard right-sided pair.

2. Split pair, edge up U R U' R'

Bring the edge over the slot, combine it with the corner, and separate or insert the pair using R moves.

3. Corner up, edge in slot U' R U R'

Match the corner to its slot color, use U moves to set up the edge, then insert with a short R trigger.

4. Corner in bottom layer R U R' U'

Align the corner beneath the target slot. Turn U to expose the edge, join the pieces, and finish with R U R'.

5. Pair in opposite slot U2 R U R' U R U2 R'

Use U2 as a setup turn, solve the visible right-hand pair, and reverse the setup to restore the face.

6. Left-hand mirror L' U' L

Mirror every right-side pattern: use L and L' for the same corner-edge pair on the left side.

Study Method

Solve all four slots naturally

1. Cross 2. White pair 3. Next slot 4. Maintain flow
Goal: complete the four first-layer slots before OLL. Recognize the pair instead of memorizing every starting position.
